# CMS Documentation

## For the Bakery Owner

Welcome to the Gul Nis Homey Cakes content management system. This is where you can update your product catalogue.

### Accessing the Studio
Navigate to `https://gulnis.in/studio` (or your configured studio path) and log in.

### Managing Products
- **Add Product**: Click on the 'Product' type and click the 'Create' button. Fill in the details (Name, Price, Category, etc.) and hit Publish.
- **Edit Product**: Click on an existing product in the list, make your changes, and hit Publish.
- **Delete Product**: Click on an existing product, click the three dots menu, and select 'Delete'.
- **Availability**: Toggle the 'Available' switch to mark a product as currently available or out of stock. If marked out of stock, customers will not see the "Order on WhatsApp" button.
- **Images**: Click on the image field to upload a new image or replace an existing one. Try to upload optimized images for better website performance.
